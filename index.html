What’s Your GOAT?

Pick the better of two items, round after round, until one is crowned the GOAT of its category. Everyone's picks add up to a community GOAT for each category.

My page

Your page is private, so no one can see your picks. Untick the box to pick a display name and get a link you can share.

Your name

Your stats

  • Yours: every matchup you have picked, by item, this session or all time.
  • Everyone: the crowd's picks across all players, head to head. 👑 counts the players who crowned an item the GOAT.
  • Agreed with the crowd: how often your pick matched the majority on the same matchup.

You can hide stats between rounds at any time. Change it with the checkbox below, or the one in the Stats panel.

Reset stats

Remove accidental picks. Removed picks also stop counting in the shared stats.
